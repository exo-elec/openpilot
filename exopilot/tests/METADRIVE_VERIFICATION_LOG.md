## Real Product Bugs Exposed & Fixed During Verification & Full `/scrutinize` Audit

1. **`selfdrive/controls/lib/aeb.py` (P0 AEB premature brake release before `AEBState.HOLD` + Scrutinize receding-lead `PRECHARGE` guard)**:
   - `_entry_plausible()` still applied `MIN_EGO_SPEED_MS (2.0 m/s)` and `MIN_CLOSING_SPEED_MS (1.0 m/s)` when `active=True`, and `_state_braking()` treated `ttc > TTC_RELEASE (4.5 s)` as `"Threat receding"` when ego slowed near a stationary obstacle (`d / v_ego > 4.5 s`), releasing brakes right in front of a stopped vehicle instead of completing the stop into `AEBState.HOLD`.
   - **Scrutinize refinement**: restricted active low-speed tracking in `_entry_plausible()` to `closing_speed > 0.0 or (threat.x <= 12.0 and threat.v_x < 0.5)` and added receding-threat abort in `_state_precharge()` so a departing lead during `PRECHARGE` returns cleanly to `IDLE` (`0.0 m/s²`) instead of escalating to `-3.0 m/s²` braking.
2. **`selfdrive/controls/lib/radar_zones.py` (`controlsd` `TypeError` crash on live capnp `stereoObjects`)**:
   - `int(obj.laneZone)` raised `TypeError: ... not 'capnp.lib.capnp._DynamicEnum'` on real pycapnp `CameraObject` messages (masked previously by integer mocks). Fixed with `_lane_zone_int(val)` reading `int(val.raw)`.
3. **`selfdrive/controls/lib/longitudinal_planner.py` (`plannerd` TLSC virtual lead + `gasPressed` override + BRSC blended-mode cap)**:
   - `_with_virtual_stop_lead()` returned a `SimpleNamespace` missing `modelProb`, `vLeadK`, `vRel` (crashed `long_mpc.py:449`). Fixed with full fields + `leadTwo` preservation, gated TLSC close-zone standstill hold on `not sm['carState'].gasPressed`, and applied BRSC positive-accel cap on `output_a_target` across both `acc` and `blended` modes.
4. **`selfdrive/controls/lib/sqsc.py` (17–28 s rough-road activation lag)**:
   - `_phase_fusion()` ramped down from the `200.0 kph` inactive sentinel instead of seeding from `max(v_ego_kph, target_limit_kph)`.
5. **`exopilot/selfdrive/gridd/occupancy.py` (Scrutinize BLOCKER: `leadsV3.v[0]` absolute `vLead` published as `vRel`)**:
   - `_fuse_modeld_detections()` assigned `vRel = lead.v[0]`, whereas `modelV2.leadsV3.v[0]` is absolute velocity (`vLead`, matching `radard.py:143`). Fixed to subtract `modelV2.velocity.x[0]` when present (`vRel = lead.v[0] - model_v_ego`).
6. **`system/inferenced/rockchip_rga.py` (Scrutinize MAJOR: packed 2D NV12 & 3D `src_uv` crash)**:
   - `_cvtcolor()` passed packed 2D NV12 `src` into `COLOR_BGR2RGB` and unpacked 3D `(h/2, w/2, 2)` `src_uv` as 2D. Fixed to route 2D NV12 to `COLOR_YUV2RGB_NV12` / `COLOR_YUV2BGR_NV12` and reshape 3D `uv`.
7. **`exopilot/selfdrive/sided/yolo_detector.py:35` & `common/core_config.py:34,39`**:
   - Fixed stale `_REPO_ROOT` (`parents[2]` -> `parents[3]`) after `exopilot/` move and updated stale `CORE_BIG`/`CORE_LITTLE` comments to match `common/realtime.py` (`LITTLE: 0-3`, `BIG: 4-7`).
8. **Inherited from `dev/NGP10` (big-model + `map_speed` + `monod` fixes)**:
   - `metadata.py` (`parents[4]`), `server_ref.py` (`OnnxBackend` fp16 cast + 0.5 s idle `dial()` stop poll), `remote_state.py` (writable `.copy()` for `Parser.softmax()`), `joining.py` (off-RT background `_join_loop` `finally:` retired-socket close), `map_speed.py` (frame-0 `gas`/`lead` TLSC clear + `sm.alive['stereoObjects']` expiry), `monod.py` (`parents[3]`).
